## 共用要求

制作“可以交给建模师”的设计板，主物件三分之四正交近景，辅以正面/侧面、剖面或小型分解结构。轮廓、厚薄、真实分件、接触点是重点；不靠换色、噪声贴图和无功能装饰堆细节。版面暖灰纸底或深灰工坊底，统一暖左主光、弱中性填光、固定曝光，深红布与深木只做小面积场景参照。木/布/纸/金属/玻璃分别表现；不要所有物件统一黄铜亮面。少量英文ID标注即可，中文正式排版在HTML中。所有尺寸为言咒自定规范，不代表Demoncards实测。

不画球头+光滑旋转体棋子，不画等距金环圆杆法杖，不画没有装配关系的漂浮零件，不画边宽如塑料壳的纸牌，不把文本框做普通统一圆角棕矩形。无商标、无Demoncards恶魔图案、无第三方游戏文字和水印。无需复制参考资产本体。

## 板 C：桌面承重、窄槽与装饰分件

覆盖TABLE-01、MAT-01、SLOT-01、DRAWER-01、CONTROL-01、DECOR-01/02/03。左半52%为桌角/窄卡槽/桌垫/抽屉局部透视与截面；右半48%为控制与书/球/沙漏四组小物件。此板有8组件，不加背景房间或角色，避免组件缩成看不清的小点。

**直接提示词：**

Create a precision fantasy tabletop component construction board for Yanzhou, landscape 16:10, finely detailed crafted props under soft warm directional light with neutral fill, no game HUD. Left 52 percent is a close three-quarter table corner assembly: dark walnut tabletop with four separate 65 mm wide and 38 mm thick edging rails, 8 mm upper and lower rounded edge transitions with a 12 mm straight middle wall, a visible 45-degree miter joint, tiny timber dowel plugs whose grain rotates, longitudinal board grain and different end grain. Show a small annotated-style cutaway without long text. On it rests 2.2 mm thick absorbent burgundy woven cloth with 3.5 mm folded edge, double flax seam and a narrow dark gold woven ribbon, not reflective metal. Show a macro of cloth fibers, seam and felt underside, clear soft contact with wood. On the cloth place one thin 9:13 paper playing card and one empty slot only 4.5 percent wider than the card, marked by four short stitched corner lines and a shallow depressed fabric impression, no full luminous rectangular frame. Below the table corner a partially open wooden materials drawer shows real dovetail side joints, two dark iron runners, a hinged brass pull with two pivot bosses, removable dividers creating three completely empty fabric-lined cells; no invented resources. Right half contains four clearly separated prop groups. CONTROL-01: four small physical controls with distinct circle/octagon/arrow bases, green insets, incised ivory-filled play, step and home glyphs, plus a three-detent speed sector, a visible press-travel seam and felt feet. DECOR-01: closed dark green leather book with five raised spine cords, recessed paper block, varied page-edge layers, only two bronze corner guards and a cloth bookmark resting on the wood. DECOR-02: translucent subdued teal glass orb with fine low-density internal crystal threads, three curved bronze claws gripping its lower third with tiny contact pads, independent supporting ring and felt feet; do not render an opaque plastic sphere or an enormous white reflection dot. DECOR-03: hourglass with octagonal wood top and bottom, three bronze support columns with threaded foot caps, two connected glass bulbs of slightly unequal outline, a thin neck and visibly separate matte gold sand cone, small scale strip on one column, no magical liquid. Show each prop complete and one small assembly or material detail. Strong readable silhouettes, authentic bevels, real layered construction, restrained wear only where hands and surfaces contact, low noise and crisp structure. Minimal labels TABLE-01 MAT-01 SLOT-01 DRAWER-01 CONTROL-01 DECOR-01 DECOR-02 DECOR-03, no extra paragraphs, no logos, no copied demon symbols, no excessive ornament, no glow, no plastic materials. This is a buildable design reference, not finished 3D assets.

复核点：木沿剖面/拼口/木纹方向可读；布边有真实厚度；卡槽只略宽于牌；抽屉不是空盒；书芯与封皮分件；晶球透层与三爪承重；沙漏砂、玻璃和柱脚分离；控制物件保留四宿主。

## 生成后交接

1. 总控把原生成文件路径、实际提示词、输出尺寸、SHA-256交给D；保持原件，不覆盖旧图。
2. D逐组件标“效果图已有／局部缺口／可交调研”；若形态没有清楚展示，保持待补，不以整体漂亮替代分件。
3. 三板与组件brief/HTML一起交调研agent：评估Higgsfield是否有实际模型/贴图/控制/版本导出收益；不得以效果图细节看起来像3D推定平台能产出可用网格。
4. 评估之后交资产生成agent，按组件独立源件、可编辑分件、单位/轴/枢轴、PBR、LOD及许可证记录交付；运行拼装须复核两档可读性、接触阴影和已定状态。
