# Anode Heart: Layer Null Analysis Outline

## Thesis

Anode Heart: Layer Null uses a loop of card collection, 40-card deckbuilding, regional BO3 battles, loss diagnosis, and deck rebuilding. The BiliSum materials strongly support combat, deckbuilding, economy, and growth-route analysis, but do not support market, retention, or full narrative conclusions.

## Target Questions

- Can this BiliSum material support a comprehensive and accurate gameplay analysis dossier?
- What are the core loop, combat resolution, deckbuilding constraints, in-run economy, and growth routes of Anode Heart: Layer Null?
- Which structures can transfer into the current unique-core-card card-battle project?
